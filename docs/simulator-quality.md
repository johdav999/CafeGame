# Stage 12: quality and maintenance

The café tracks espresso-machine integrity and cleanliness on an integer 0-1000 scale. Every started brew reduces integrity by 100 and cleanliness by 150 exactly once, using its production-job ID as a durable watermark. Cancelling before production starts causes no wear; cancelling after starting retains wear and ingredient consumption. Brewing requires both metrics to be at least 300 and no active maintenance task. Product base quality is the lower pre-use metric.

Coffee stays fresh for 45 simulation seconds from the actual production-completion deadline. Its displayed quality decreases linearly from base quality to zero. At the exact expiry boundary the order is abandoned, the cup is discarded, and no sale is paid. Cancelled or abandoned cups are also discarded rather than accumulating unowned output. Waste is counted, and atomic Runtime::remove only removes unreserved stock. Core claims are released through normal abandonment before disposal. Serving checks freshness again before creating a payment.

Clean machine creates a free two-second barista task that restores cleanliness. Repair machine creates a four-second task, prepaid at 5.00, that restores integrity. Both use the existing TaskBoard at priority 30 and require an idle service order. They wait for an already active staff task, reserve the machine through the game command gate, and navigate to its interaction point through existing engine paths. Cancel maintenance refunds a pending repair in full and applies no completed effect; repeat cancellation cannot refund again. Once completed a repair is nonrefundable. Staff breaks preserve remaining work and requeue it with fresh tokens; global pause and hidden-tab suspension stop simulation time. Wages continue while the barista is on shift, including maintenance and walking.

The HUD shows condition, cleanliness, cup quality and freshness, waste and maintenance progress. The scene shows residue on a dirty machine and an indicator when integrity blocks use; maintenance uses the existing staff work prop. Finances includes net repair costs and repair refunds. Coffee still sells for 5.00 regardless of score; satisfaction-based pricing belongs to a later stage. Ingredient-lot ageing, temperature, random failures and richer repair tooling are future game integrations; the generic Freshness type can represent other perishable products.

The engine adds optional engine/simulator/quality.hpp with Freshness and Condition. Freshness validates a positive bounded shelf life and computes deterministic integer samples from explicit simulation time. Condition supplies saturating wear/dirt, independent clean/repair restoration, typed state validation and monotonic use-source protection. Both are portable value mechanisms without scene, clock, inventory, task or ledger ownership. Runtime::remove atomically disposes a positive resource bundle while preserving every existing stock/capacity hold and event-budget atomicity.

Envelope schema 6 appends condition, use watermark, batch quality, freshness origin, waste and maintenance identity/type/cost. The joint loader cross-checks core order/job state, product completion time, task roles/durations/ownership, finance totals and repair eligibility before publishing the candidate. Formats 1-5 migrate with a fully maintained machine and a recorded watermark for any already-started job, so loading never causes retroactive wear. A ready legacy cup retains its real production time and can expire on the next simulation boundary. Core snapshot schema 1, the renderer pin and the localStorage slot remain unchanged.

The diagnostic console host adds clean-machine, repair and cancel-care commands. Generic tests cover freshness boundaries/overflow, repeated use sources, independent restoration, atomic mixed-bundle disposal and reservation/outbox pressure. Café tests cover expiry without pay, wear-once restore, cleaning/repair interruption, refunds, blocked equipment, failed affordability, invalid saves and migration.
