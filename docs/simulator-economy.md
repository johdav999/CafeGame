# Stage 11: cash, costs and wages

New browser shifts start with 50.00 cash and four beans. Espresso sells for 5.00. Each bean costs 1.00; a twelve-bean order costs 12.00 up front and reserves storage. Cancelling an incoming delivery refunds its prepaid amount once. Delivered goods remain paid for. Manual and automatic purchases use the same affordability checks, and automatic ordering waits for cash rather than creating debt or repeated charges.

The barista earns 1.00 per 60 seconds on shift, including idle time. Staff breaks stop wage accrual. Global pause and tab suspension stop all simulation time. Fractional wage progress survives save/load. Whole wages are paid from cash when due. If cash runs out, unpaid wages remain visible and future coffee income pays them before new supplies. The game keeps cash nonnegative and reserves unpaid wages when checking purchasing power.

The Cash display uses two decimal places. Finances shows sales, net supply costs after refunds, paid wages, operating profit and unpaid wages, plus the six most recent transactions. Profit excludes opening cash. The portable ledger retains 128 recent entries and archives older amounts and stream watermarks, so totals and duplicate protection continue across long sessions. The primitive cafe art direction and both browser renderer paths are unchanged.

The engine provides optional engine/simulator/economy.hpp: a bounded integer Ledger with signed postings, per-stream sequence watermarks, nonnegative cash, prepared publication, compact history and validated typed snapshots. CafeGame owns the prices, opening cash, refund eligibility, staffed-time accrual and wage payment order. A diagnostic Simulation may choose its own starting stock and opening cash; the browser and console example explicitly select four beans and 50.00.

Purchases prepare a ledger candidate before reserving incoming inventory; sales prepare before delivering coffee. With exclusive ledger ownership and no callbacks between those operations, successful core changes are followed by allocation-free ledger publication. A failed stock reservation or rejected posting leaves the cash and stock command unchanged. Pending core receipts are still applied and acknowledged once using the saved receipt watermark.

Save envelope 5 appends the ledger, its archived summaries, staffed time, legacy sales baseline and pending prepaid amount to the existing supply/workflow/spatial/core state. The loader validates the entire candidate before publication, including cash, sales sequence, wage totals, debt and pending cost. Formats 1-4 migrate: existing cash becomes opening cash, historical sales remain represented by their legacy baseline, wage accrual starts on upgrade, and old in-flight deliveries remain free. No historical transaction list is fabricated. Schema 5 allows the legacy sales baseline plus the bounded new sales total, so migrated long-running games remain loadable. The core Runtime snapshot stays schema 1; the renderer pin and localStorage slot remain unchanged.

The diagnostic console host supports order, restock, supplies-cancel, auto and break along with existing service/save commands, and reports cash, profit and wage debt in minor units. Pricing controls, taxes, loans, rent, depreciation, multi-currency accounts, bankruptcies and a full accounting system remain future work.
